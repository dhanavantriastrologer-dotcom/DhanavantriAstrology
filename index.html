The Essence of Jyotishyam
Jyotishyam: A Guiding Light
Ancient Wisdom, Human Effort & Divine Timing

Jyotishyam is not merely superstition; it is an ancient knowledge system that studies the relationship between planetary movements, time, karma, and human life.

At the time of birth, the position of the planets is believed to reflect certain tendencies, strengths, challenges, and possibilities in a person’s life.

A horoscope is not a final judgment of destiny; it is a map that helps us understand the path, timing, and lessons of life.

Planets may indicate a direction, but they do not completely control human life. Our karma, decisions, discipline, and effort shape the final outcome.

A strong human will, sincere effort, dharmic living, and self-awareness can reduce, transform, or overcome many difficult planetary influences.

Jyotishyam should not create fear; it should create awareness, clarity, preparation, and confidence to live life with wisdom.

Before You Visit
Guidelines & Dress Code
👗
Women's Dress Code
Wear traditional attire — saree or salwar kameez. Hair must be neatly tied. Bangles should be worn. Avoid Western clothing.
🧔
Men's Dress Code
Wear clean, modest clothing. Dhoti or formal wear preferred. Remove footwear before entering. No shorts or sleeveless.
🕐
Visiting Hours
Morning session: 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM. Evening session: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM. No consultations on amavasya and marked days.
🙏
Conduct & Respect
Maintain silence. No phones during consultation. Speak truthfully. Come with a pure heart and respectful intent.
📋
What to Bring
Birth date, birth time, and birth place of the person. For marriage queries, bring details of both individuals.
🚫
Not Permitted
No recording of any kind. No arguments or confrontation. Children below 10 years should be accompanied by an adult.

Appointments cannot be booked on the following days. The system will automatically block these.
Amavasya (New Moon) Sundays Ekadashi Karthika Masam (no evenings) Festival Days
