The Astrologer

Sri Dhanvantri Sharma

Vedic Scholar & Jyotishi

Sri Dhanvantri Sharma has been serving the community for over three decades, offering guidance rooted in traditional Vedic astrology and sacred scriptures.

His consultations cover life events, marriage compatibility, career direction, health concerns, and family welfare — always guided by dharma.

Consultations are conducted in-person only. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time. Bring a valid ID and your birth details.

Appointments are accepted one day in advance only. Same-day walk-ins are not guaranteed. Please book early to secure your slot.

Before You Visit

Guidelines & Dress Code

👗
Women's Dress Code
Wear traditional attire — saree or salwar kameez. Hair must be neatly tied. Bangles should be worn. Avoid Western clothing.
🧔
Men's Dress Code
Wear clean, modest clothing. Dhoti or formal wear preferred. Remove footwear before entering. No shorts or sleeveless.
🕐
Visiting Hours
Morning session: 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM. Evening session: 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM. No consultations on amavasya and marked days.
🙏
Conduct & Respect
Maintain silence. No phones during consultation. Speak truthfully. Come with a pure heart and respectful intent.
📋
What to Bring
Birth date, birth time, and birth place of the person. For marriage queries, bring details of both individuals.
🚫
Not Permitted
No recording of any kind. No arguments or confrontation. Children below 10 years should be accompanied by an adult.
⚠️ No Consultations on These Days
Appointments cannot be booked on the following days. The system will automatically block these.
Amavasya (New Moon) Sundays Ekadashi Karthika Masam (no evenings) Festival Days
